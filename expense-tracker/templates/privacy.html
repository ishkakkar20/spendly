Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address and password. As you use the service, we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and notes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to contact you about your account. We do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely and passwords are never kept in plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of Spendly and its users.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to make the service work. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising or tracking.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You can view and edit your expense data at any time. You may also ask us to correct or delete your account and all associated data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Spendly after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
